# 03. Random Variables, Law of Large Numbers, and Central Limit Theorem

## 1. Overview
This module explores expectation, variance properties of discrete and continuous random variables, and empirically simulates the Law of Large Numbers (LLN) and Central Limit Theorem (CLT) starting from a non-normal (Exponential) distribution.

## 2. Mathematical Formulas
- **Expected Value & Variance Properties:**
  $$E[aX + bY + c] = aE[X] + bE[Y] + c$$
  $$\text{Var}(aX + b) = a^2 \text{Var}(X)$$
  $$\text{Var}(X + Y) = \text{Var}(X) + \text{Var}(Y) + 2\text{Cov}(X, Y)$$

- **Weak Law of Large Numbers (WLLN):**
  $$\lim_{n \to \infty} P\left(|\bar{X}_n - \mu| \ge \epsilon\right) = 0$$

- **Central Limit Theorem (Lindeberg-Lévy CLT):**
  $$Z_n = \frac{\bar{X}_n - \mu}{\sigma / \sqrt{n}} \xrightarrow{d} N(0, 1) \quad \text{as } n \to \infty$$

## 3. Implementation Summary
- Computes theoretical expectation and variance for an Exponential distribution $\text{Exp}(\lambda)$.
- Simulates sample means $\bar{X}_n$ across 10,000 replications for various sample sizes $n \in \{5, 30, 100\}$.
- Calculates standardized values and verifies standard normal moments (mean $\approx 0$, variance $\approx 1$, skewness $\approx 0$).
