# 07. Paired Samples t-Test

## 1. Overview
When observations are matched or repeated measures (e.g., pre-test vs. post-test on the same subjects), observations are correlated. The paired $t$-test converts bivariate paired data into a univariate series of differences $d_i = x_{1i} - x_{2i}$.

## 2. Mathematical Formulas
- **Differences:**
  $$d_i = x_{1i} - x_{2i}, \quad i = 1, \dots, n$$

- **Mean and Variance of Differences:**
  $$\bar{d} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n d_i, \quad s_d^2 = \frac{1}{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^n (d_i - \bar{d})^2$$

- **Paired $t$-statistic:**
  $$t = \frac{\bar{d} - \mu_d}{s_d / \sqrt{n}} \sim t(n-1)$$

## 3. Implementation Summary
- Computes element-wise differences $d$, followed by mean, standard error of difference, and $t$-distribution lookup.
- Validates against `t.test(x, y, paired = TRUE)` and one-sample test on differences `t.test(x - y)`.
