# 02. Probability Basics and Classical Definition

## 1. Overview
This module demonstrates classical probability models (Laplace definition) and the empirical/frequentist definition of probability via Monte Carlo simulation (Law of Large Numbers demonstration on events). It calculates exact combinatorial probabilities and compares them with empirical relative frequencies.

## 2. Mathematical Formulas
- **Classical Probability (Equally Likely Outcomes):**
  $$P(A) = \frac{|A|}{|\Omega|} = \frac{\text{Number of favorable outcomes}}{\text{Total number of possible outcomes}}$$

- **Empirical Probability (Relative Frequency Limit):**
  $$P_n(A) = \frac{n_A}{n} \xrightarrow{n \to \infty} P(A)$$

- **Conditional Probability & Bayes' Rule:**
  $$P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)} = \frac{P(B \mid A) P(A)}{P(B)}$$

## 3. Implementation Summary
- Computes classical probability for rolling a sum of 7 with two fair 6-sided dice ($|A| / |\Omega|$).
- Simulates $n = 100,000$ dice rolls and computes convergence of relative frequencies.
- Evaluates Bayes' Theorem manually and verifies against conditional sample filtering.
